Questions we hear every week

Will my insurance pay for a new windshield?
Often, yes. Many comprehensive policies cover glass, and some have a low or $0 glass deductible. Give us your carrier and policy details and we'll check your coverage, file the claim and bill the insurer directly.
Can you replace my windshield at my home or job?
Yes. Our mobile service comes to you anywhere in our service area. Most replacements take about an hour, plus time for the adhesive to cure before you drive.
Do you recalibrate the camera behind my windshield?
Many newer vehicles have lane-assist and braking cameras mounted on the windshield that need recalibration after replacement. Confirm with client: in-house ADAS calibration or a partner shop.
My storefront glass is broken. How fast can you get here?
Call 662-843-4513 any time, day or night. We secure the opening first so your business is safe, then schedule the permanent glass.
Do you install frameless shower doors?
Yes. We measure on site, help you choose glass thickness and hardware, and install the finished enclosure. Custom mirrors can be ordered for the same bathroom.
